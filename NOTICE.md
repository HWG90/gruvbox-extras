# Third-party material and provenance

This repository is a **personal desktop theme**, not a redistribution of anyone's
assets. It is kept private for that reason.

## Trademarks and game artwork

Every glyph depicts a mark owned by its rights holder. Warhammer 40,000, Space
Marine, DOOM, Quake, Hades, Metal Gear, Resident Evil, Selaco, Trepang2,
Ultrakill, Beyond Citadel, Dead Space, The Blood of Dawnwalker and HELLDIVERS are
trademarks of their respective owners (Games Workshop, id Software, Bethesda,
Supergiant Games, Konami, Capcom, and their respective studios). No affiliation
with, or endorsement by, any of them is claimed.

## Where the glyphs come from

| Glyph | Source |
|---|---|
| wordmark glyphs | the game's own `logo.png` in the local Steam `librarycache` |
| icon-art glyphs | the installed `steam_icon_<appid>.png` (hicolor theme) |
| DOOM: TDA crest | composited from the installed icon art, re-toned to gruvbox |
| Ultramarines U | Warhammer 40k – Lexicanum chapter badge, kept at `sets/flat/sources/ultramarines-badge.png` |

Steam CDN cover art was downloaded during development for comparison but is **not
committed here** (it is not ours to redistribute). The URLs, if a rebuild ever
needs them, are:

```
https://cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/<appid>/library_600x900_2x.jpg
```
